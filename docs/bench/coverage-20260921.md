## What the gaps actually are

Three kinds, and they do not deserve the same response.

### 1. Untested, with no excuse — `trellis-cli/src/main.rs` at 39.09%

**`crates/trellis-cli` has no `tests/` directory.** Its only tests are three
`#[cfg(test)]` modules inside `main.rs` (`interrupt_tests`, `doctor_tests`,
`init_tests`) that exercise extracted helpers. Every command entry point —
`cmd_index`, `cmd_check`, `cmd_radius`, `cmd_run`, `cmd_resume`, `cmd_doctor`,
`cmd_log`, `cmd_why`, `cmd_models`, `cmd_partitions` — is reached from exactly
one place, the dispatch in `main`, and from no test at all.

This is the finding that matters most, because the quality audit's rule is
*"mutation score must not drop"* and a mutation score cannot drop from zero.
The audit's Pass 5 wants to split `main.rs` by command, which is precisely the
refactor that rule exists to guard, and the guard is currently blind there.

Fixed by `crates/trellis-cli/tests/cli.rs` — see CORRECTNESS_LEDGER.md.

### 2. Needs a TTY — `tui/` at 75.32%

`tui::run`, `event_loop`, `on_mouse`, `install_log_forwarding`,
`TerminalGuard::terminal` and the two spawned threads need a real terminal
and a real crossterm event stream. `model.rs` (97.33%) and `view.rs` (90.00%)
are the parts deliberately factored to be testable without one, and they are
tested; the uncovered remainder is the I/O shell that separation was for.

A test harness for this would mean a pty, and the thing it would test is
crossterm's contract rather than ours. Left uncovered, on purpose, and the
architecture is what makes that an acceptable answer rather than an excuse.

### 3. Needs something this machine does not have

- `trellis-loop/src/adapter.rs` (77.53% lines, **61.90% functions**) — the
  uncovered half is `RuntimeModel`'s `Model` impl, which needs a live runtime
  on `127.0.0.1`. The tier-selection and JSON-extraction logic around it is
  tested; the transport is not.
- `trellis-model/src/runtime.rs` (79.69% functions) — `probe_slots`, `get`,
  `with_timeout`: the same boundary.
- `trellis-vcs`: `Worktree::create` / `Worktree::discard` are **dead code**,
  written ahead of need for 4.13's per-partition isolation and called from
  nowhere. Not merely untested — unreferenced. Recorded as a finding rather
  than deleted, because v1.1 plans to use them and the decision is about
  sequencing, not correctness.
- `trellis-core/src/ids.rs` at 70% is three lines in a newtype accessor, and
  `PartitionId::index` is unused until partitions are handed to agents.

## A note on reading the function-level numbers

The `--json` export lists one entry per *instantiation per object file*, so a
naive "functions with count 0" query reports `SymbolId::index` and
`run_seeded` as never executed — both plainly false, the first because the
same function appears in several compilation units with different crate
disambiguators, the second because an `async fn`'s outer stub is accounted
separately from its generator.

Deduplicating on the demangled name and taking the maximum count gives 1,078
distinct functions, 160 never executed, of which 70 are named source
functions rather than closures or derived impls. Those 70 are what the three
sections above describe. The per-file line percentages in the table need no
such correction and are the figures to quote.
